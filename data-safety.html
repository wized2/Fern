Data safety (Play Console guide)

Suggested answers when filling Google Play Data safety for com.endroid.fern:

  • Does your app collect or share user data? No (if you ship without analytics — current Fern design).
  • Encrypted in transit: N/A if no collection.
  • Users can request deletion: N/A — no cloud account data.
  • Independent security review: No (unless you obtain one later).

Permissions such as notification, battery, or usage access are for on-device features only. Document each permission’s purpose in the Play Console declarations.
