Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · App: Fern (com.endroid.fern)

Summary

Fern is a local system monitor. It does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or account login. Metrics are computed on your device and are not uploaded by Fern to our servers.

Data Fern accesses on the device

  • System metrics (CPU, memory, storage, battery, network counters, thermal where available)
  • Optional: list of running apps and approximate resource use when you enable advanced features
  • Optional: display-over-other-apps for the floating island overlay
  • Optional: Shizuku or root only if you explicitly enable advanced mode

Data collection & sharing

Fern does not collect personal information for advertising or analytics. Fern does not sell data. If you download the app from Google Play or GitHub, those platforms process download/update traffic under their own policies.

Internet

Core monitoring works offline. Optional update checks may contact GitHub Releases or Play to see if a new version exists. No telemetry batch is sent by Fern itself.

Children

Fern is a general utility, not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can deny optional permissions, disable advanced mode, or uninstall Fern at any time. Uninstalling removes app-local preferences stored on the device.
