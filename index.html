Your phone’s pulse, at a glance.

Fern is a clean system monitor for Android — CPU, RAM, thermal, battery, storage, and active apps. No ads. No accounts. Fully offline after install.

What Fern tracks

Live gauges on Home, a readable hardware snapshot in Details, and optional elevated access when you want more.

CPU & RAM

Live gauges, load estimates, memory used vs total, and trend sparklines on Home.

Thermal

Battery temperature and thermal status — Advanced mode can dig into real zones via Shizuku or root.

Battery & storage

Charge level, temperature, and internal storage — quick bars, no clutter.

Active apps

Recently in the foreground. Optional Usage access; data stays on the device.

More tools

Tests, sensors, settings, notch island overlay, and About — all under one tab.

Private by design

No internet permission for core monitoring. No tracking. Advanced mode only if you turn it on.

Screenshots

Five frames. Same calm green — day or night.
