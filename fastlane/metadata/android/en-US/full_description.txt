Fern is a private system monitor for Android.

• Live CPU, RAM, battery, storage, network
• Material You colors, light and dark themes
• Optional Dynamic Island–style overlay
• Advanced mode via Shizuku or root for accurate CPU and per-app stats
• No ads, no analytics, works offline

Privacy policy: https://get-fern.vercel.app/privacy.html
